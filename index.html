About Lana

Fourteen years of photographing families across Fife.

From a newborn's first days to first birthdays and everything in between, Lana has spent over a decade getting to know the families she photographs. Every session happens in her own home studio in Kirkcaldy — a relaxed, unhurried space built around your family, not a shot list.

No rushing, no stiff poses — just time and patience, so the pictures look like you.
